### **Recipe Overview**
*   **Yield:** 16 XL cookies or 20 medium/large cookies
*   **Prep time:** 15 minutes
*   **Cook time:** 13 minutes
*   **Total time:** 2 hours, 45 minutes (includes chilling)

---

### **Ingredients**
*   **2 and 1/4 cups (281g)** all-purpose flour (spooned & leveled)
*   **1 teaspoon** baking soda
*   **1 and 1/2 teaspoons** cornstarch
*   **1/2 teaspoon** salt
*   **3/4 cup (170g/12 Tbsp)** unsalted butter, melted and cooled for 5 minutes
*   **3/4 cup (150g)** packed light or dark brown sugar
*   **1/2 cup (100g)** granulated sugar
*   **1 large egg + 1 egg yolk**, at room temperature
*   **2 teaspoons** pure vanilla extract
*   **1 and 1/4 cups (225g)** semi-sweet chocolate chips or chocolate chunks

---

### **Instructions**
1.  **Mix Dry Ingredients:** In a large bowl, whisk the flour, baking soda, cornstarch, and salt together. Set aside.
2.  **Mix Wet Ingredients:** In a medium bowl, whisk the melted butter, brown sugar, and granulated sugar together until no lumps remain. Whisk in the egg and egg yolk, then the vanilla extract.
3.  **Combine:** Pour the wet ingredients into the dry ingredients and mix with a large spoon or spatula. The dough will be soft, thick, and shiny. Fold in the chocolate chips.
4.  **Chill:** Cover the dough tightly and refrigerate for **at least 2 hours** (up to 3 days). Chilling overnight is highly recommended to prevent overspreading.
5.  **Prepare for Baking:** Preheat oven to **325°F (163°C)**. Line baking sheets with parchment paper or silicone mats. If the dough was chilled longer than 2 hours, let it sit at room temperature for 15 minutes to soften slightly.
6.  **Shape:** Scoop the dough (approx. 3 scant Tablespoons for XL cookies or 2 heaping Tablespoons for medium). Roll into a ball, then shape it to be **taller rather than wide** (like a cylinder) to ensure thickness. Place 3 inches apart on sheets.
7.  **Bake:** Bake for **13–14 minutes**. Edges should be very lightly browned; the centers will still look very soft. 
8.  **Cool:** Let cookies cool on the baking sheet for 10 minutes before moving to a wire rack to cool completely.

---

### **Essential Notes**
*   **Cornstarch:** If you do not have it, it can be omitted, though it helps with softness.
*   **Egg Temperature:** To bring eggs to room temperature quickly, place them in a bowl of warm water for 5 minutes.
*   **Storage:** Store at room temperature for up to 1 week.
*   **Freezing:** Unbaked dough balls or baked cookies can be frozen for up to 3 months. Bake frozen dough balls for an extra minute (no thawing required).
